About the showroom

A showroom the whole family can walk into.

I opened LK Garments in Malsisar because people here had to travel to the city for a good choice of clothes, and usually for ladies and gents separately. I wanted one clean, well-lit place where everyone in the family finds something they like.

Since we started in 2022, regular customers have become like extended family. Many come every festival and wedding season, and they know I will tell them honestly what suits them and what does not.

Come in, look around without any pressure, and if you want, message me on WhatsApp first and I will show you what has just arrived.

  • Ladies and gents collection together in one showroom
  • Bright, clean, air-conditioned-feel shop with easy browsing
  • Fixed, honest prices with no bargaining games
  • Fresh stock for every wedding and festival season
  • Owner is available on call and WhatsApp
